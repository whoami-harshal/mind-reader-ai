# Mind Reader AI — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 26 October 2026*

Mind Reader AI is a 20-questions guessing game that runs inside Anna. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: it only uses what it needs to play the game, and it keeps your game data in your own Anna account.

## What is sent to Anna's AI

To play, the app sends the game so far to Anna's AI service through Anna's built-in AI feature:

- the category and game language you picked,
- the questions asked and the answers you tapped or typed,
- the AI's earlier guesses, and
- if you beat the AI, the answer you type in ("What were you thinking of?").

This is used only to produce the next question, guess or reply. The app has no servers of its own and does not send your data anywhere else. Anna's own handling of AI requests is covered by Anna's privacy policy.

**Please don't type personal information** (like a friend's full name, a phone number or an address) into the game. If you do type a private person's name, the app will not save it to your stats or put it in share text.

## What is stored, and where

The app saves a small amount of data in **your own Anna storage**, which only you and this app can use:

- your settings (app language, game language, night/light theme),
- your game stats (games played, wins, fastest read, hardest stump, today's record),
- an unfinished game, so you can continue after closing the app (deleted when the game ends or you quit), and
- the last 20 secrets the AI picked in "You read my mind", so it doesn't repeat itself.

The app does **not** collect your name, contacts, location, photos or device identifiers. It has **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you choose to. When you tap **Copy** or **Share** on the result screen, the share text goes only where you send it.

## Deleting your data

- **Stats:** open **Settings → Reset stats**.
- **An unfinished game:** tap **Quit** (or **Give up**) — the saved game is deleted.
- **Everything:** remove Mind Reader AI from your Anna account, or contact us below and we'll explain how.

## Children

The game is family-friendly: the AI is instructed to keep every question and answer suitable for all ages, and offensive words typed into the game are never repeated or saved.
